How placeholders work

Put a name in curly braces and it is filled in automatically, or asked for when you click the button.

  • Filled automatically: {meeting} {chair} {now} {today} {item} {item_speaker} {item_duration} {item_notes} {prev_item} {next_item} {remaining} {return_time}
  • Asked when sending: anything else, e.g. {reason}. It becomes a box, labelled from the name.
  • Choices: {minutes:5|10|15|30} gives one-tap buttons plus “Other…”.
  • Optional: {reason?} — a line like “Reason: {reason?}” disappears if left empty.
  • Names ending in date, time or deadline get date/time pickers. {return_time} uses {minutes}.
